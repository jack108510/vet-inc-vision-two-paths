# 02 Two Paths

**Intended audience:** General public — any veterinary operator who lands on the Vet INC homepage without prior knowledge of which product applies to them. Works equally for a multi-site corporate buyer and an independent clinic owner.

**Design posture:** Elegant, neutral, and architecturally clear. Warm off-white background (`#F8F6F2`), with two distinct accent systems: navy (`#1B3660`) for the enterprise/groups path and forest green (`#2A6347`) for the clinic/FeeWatch path. The split is visual and immediate — the first viewport communicates the two-track structure without requiring the visitor to read a paragraph of explanation first.

**Positioning emphasis:** The shared foundation of both offers (your data, your decisions, authorization before action) is surfaced in a dedicated section so the two products read as siblings rather than unrelated tools. The paths are differentiated by audience and product scope, not by brand.

**Key guardrails honoured:**
- Both paths carry inline scope qualifications at the right depth (not over-hedged).
- No fabricated testimonials, metrics, logos, or pricing.
- The "honest note" band consolidates the causal caveat for both products in one place.
- FeeWatch and the enterprise product are described accurately — no automatic recommendations, no live PMS writeback.

**Sentinel:** `VISION-CONCEPT-02` (HTML comment in `<head>` + visually-hidden `<span>` in `<body>`).
