Veterinary groups

Controlled implementation of approved price changes across your group

For corporate operators and multi-site groups, Vet INC provides a structured authorization-to-implementation workflow. Every fee change is explicitly approved, mapped to PMS catalogue entries, released in controlled rollouts, and recorded with a full audit trail.

  • Stakeholder authorization before any change proceeds
  • PMS catalogue mapping across all locations
  • Exception management and rollout status tracking
  • Tamper-evident audit history for every action
  • Controlled PMS writeback (planned; contractually governed)
Talk to us about your group

Live PMS writeback is in development and not yet available. All implementation scopes are agreed individually.

Independent clinics

FeeWatch: historical context for your pricing-review cadence

For independent practices and small groups, FeeWatch adds historical context to your fee reviews by studying your clinic’s own sales, revenue, and visit records. It supports a manageable, recurring review routine—it does not set prices or make automatic recommendations.

  • Historical context drawn from your own data
  • Supports a regular pricing-review cadence
  • Sales, revenue, and visit records in context
  • Decisions remain with your team, on your schedule
Ask about FeeWatch

FeeWatch provides historical context to inform decisions. It does not guarantee outcomes or replace clinical or financial judgement.

Our approach

A shared discipline, whatever your scale

Both products are built on the same principle: pricing decisions belong to your team, and the tools should give them better information and more control—not less.

☐

Authorization before action

Nothing changes without explicit sign-off from the people responsible for it. The system enforces the sequence; it does not skip steps.

📄

Your data, your decisions

Vet INC uses the records your clinics already generate. There are no external benchmarks, no manufactured metrics, and no claim that the tool makes decisions for you.

📊

Context, not conclusions

The output of both products is better-informed people, not automated outcomes. Pricing requires judgement; the tools support it.

📋

Audit and record

Every significant action is recorded. Whether you are managing a ten-clinic group or a single practice, you should be able to see what happened and when.

A note on what these tools do not do: Neither product replaces your pricing, finance, or analytics team, makes automatic price changes, or guarantees specific outcomes. FeeWatch surfaces historical patterns in your own data—what your team does with that context is your decision. The enterprise platform enforces a controlled workflow; it does not make choices on your behalf.
